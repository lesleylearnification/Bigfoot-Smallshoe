# Bigfoot Smallshoe Build 6
## Module 6 of 10: Field Guide Cabinet and Research Discovery

This module turns the research library into an explorable in-world archive.

### Completed

- Physical wooden Field Guide Cabinet
- Five behavior-specific drawers
- Source artifacts presented as:
  - field journals
  - ranger training memos
  - library checkout cards
  - folded conference handouts
  - archive copies
- Complete existing research bank integration
- Bigfoot’s note explaining why each source matters
- A practical note explaining what the player can improve
- Direct external source links
- Saved Field Notes pouch
- Research discovery persistence
- Cabin access to the cabinet
- Reflection-card access to the relevant drawer
- Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile cabinet layouts

### Playtest focus

1. Does research feel discovered rather than assigned?
2. Are players curious enough to open more than one source?
3. Is the correct drawer easy to understand?
4. Are Bigfoot’s notes useful and in character?
5. Does the Saved Field Notes feature make research feel collectible without turning it into a generic achievement system?
6. Can players move between reflection and research without losing their place?

### Remaining modules

7. The Forest Remembers, progression, persistence, and endings
8. Animation, sound, tactile transitions, and environmental storytelling
9. Responsive layouts, accessibility, testing, and performance
10. Final integration, QA, documentation, and GitHub release package
