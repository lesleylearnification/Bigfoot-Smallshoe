# Bigfoot Smallshoe Build 6
## Module 7 of 10: The Forest Remembers, Progression, Persistence, and Endings

This module implements the game’s central thesis: recognition changes culture.

### Completed

- Five-stage cultural progression
- Evolving cabin window and forest
- Persistent multi-round state
- Quiet Legends collection
- Constellation Wall and memory network
- Behavior-specific progression totals
- Evolving Linda and Gary commentary
- Culture-stage narrative feedback
- Dynamic recognition profile
- Three ending states:
  - The Forest That Learned to Notice
  - Legend of the Pines
  - The Forest of Missed Opportunities
- Hidden culture-sustains-itself ending
- Season reset and replay loop

### How progression works

Complete five expeditions to reach an ending. Strong expert-aligned decisions add recognition echoes to The Forest Remembers. As echoes accumulate, the cabin, forest, Linda, Gary, and team culture visibly change.

### Playtest focus

1. Do players notice that the cabin and forest change?
2. Is the Constellation Wall understandable without explanation?
3. Do Quiet Legends feel emotionally meaningful rather than like trophies?
4. Does Linda and Gary’s evolution make the world feel persistent?
5. Does the hidden ending communicate that a strong culture no longer depends on one manager?
6. Does the ending reflect how the player actually played?
