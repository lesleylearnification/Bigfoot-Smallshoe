# Bigfoot Smallshoe Build 6
## Module 4 of 10: Scenario Journal and Gameplay Engine

This module adds the complete playable decision loop.

### Completed

- Interactive Smallshoe Forest map
- Five named locations
- Trail Journal gameplay screen
- One randomized scenario from each of five recognition behaviors
- All 25 existing recognition scenarios
- Four plausible, similarly structured choices per scenario
- Hidden multidimensional scoring
- Narrative Forest Signs instead of exposed percentages
- Behavior-specific motifs and field-sketch illustrations
- Evidence collection and persistent Evidence Satchel
- Five-step trail progress
- Ranger margin notes
- Sealed-choice flow
- Round-complete summary
- Local browser persistence

### Playtest focus

1. Are the choices plausible enough to require judgment?
2. Is the correct choice less obvious than in earlier builds?
3. Does the journal feel like a physical artifact rather than a form?
4. Is the current recognition behavior easy to understand?
5. Does the Evidence Satchel make progress feel tangible?
6. Can players comfortably complete the flow on desktop, tablet, and mobile?
