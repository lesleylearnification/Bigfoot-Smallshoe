This module adds the sensory and tactile layer of the Art Lock build.

  - cabin ambience
  - forest ambience
  - page turn
  - drawer slide
  - footprint stamp
  - pinned note
  - kettle
- Page-turn transitions between major screens

- Research artifact lift feedback

### Browser note

Modern browsers require a user gesture before ambience can begin. Sound activates after the player first clicks or presses a key.
