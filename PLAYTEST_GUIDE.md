# Bigfoot Smallshoe Playtest Guide

## Recommended playtest length

- First-impression test: 5 minutes
- One-expedition test: 10 to 15 minutes
- Full progression test: five expeditions across multiple sessions

## Observe without helping

During the first test, do not explain the interface. Watch whether the player can:

1. Identify that they are playing as Bigfoot
2. Understand that the Trail Journal begins the game
3. Select a location
4. Make and seal a choice
5. Understand the current recognition behavior
6. Complete all five scenarios
7. Rate My Trail before viewing the Expert Trail
8. Find the Field Guide Cabinet
9. Understand that the forest changes over time

## Ask afterward

### Immersion
- Did this feel like a game, a website, or training?
- What made the cabin feel believable or unbelievable?
- Did you feel like you were playing as Bigfoot?

### Gameplay
- Which choice was hardest?
- Were any correct answers obvious?
- Did the Evidence Satchel and trail progress help?

### Learning
- What recognition behavior do you remember most?
- What would you do differently as a manager?
- Did the expert comparison change your understanding?

### Emotion and humor
- Did anything make you laugh?
- Did you care about Bigfoot?
- Did the ending feel earned?

### Curiosity
- Which drawer or object did you most want to inspect?
- Did you open more than one research source?
- Did you notice any quiet discoveries?

### Accessibility
- Was any text difficult to read?
- Were touch targets comfortable?
- Did sound or motion ever become distracting?

## Success indicators

- “I forgot I was using a browser.”
- “I wanted to open every drawer.”
- “The choices made me think.”
- “I will probably try that at work.”
- “I liked Bigfoot.”
- “I wanted another expedition.”
