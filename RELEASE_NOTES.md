# Bigfoot Smallshoe 6.0
## Art Lock Release

**Helping everyone. Seen by no one.**

Build 6.0 is the vision-complete playable prototype of Bigfoot Smallshoe. It rebuilds the earlier web prototype around a handcrafted cabin, physical field-journal interactions, meaningful recognition practice, expert comparison, research discovery, persistent cultural progression, and multiple endings.

## Core experience

Players become Bigfoot Smallshoe, an anonymous forest guardian who helps people receive meaningful recognition while avoiding personal fame.

A complete expedition includes:

1. Choosing a trail on the Smallshoe Forest map
2. Completing one scenario for each recognition behavior
3. Sealing five decisions in the Trail Journal
4. Comparing My Trail with the Expert Trail
5. Rating personal judgment with five Bigfoot footprints
6. Reading the Ranger Report and newspaper outcome
7. Choosing a practical next-expedition commitment
8. Watching the cabin, characters, and forest change over time

## Five recognition behaviors

- Notice overlooked contributions
- Make recognition specific
- Recognize fairly
- Keep recognition meaningful and earned
- Personalize recognition

- 25 randomized scenarios

- Expert sources and linked research
- Five-footprint self-ratings
- Field Guide Cabinet
- Saved Field Notes
- The Forest Remembers
- Quiet Legends

- Three ending paths, including a hidden culture-sustains-itself ending

## Art Lock direction

The interface uses physical objects rather than generic software components:

- Cabin as menu
- Journal as gameplay interface
- Cabinet as research library
- Ranger notebook as feedback
- Constellation Wall as progression
- Newspaper and field artifacts as outcomes

- No external runtime dependencies
- GitHub Pages ready
- Responsive down to 320 pixels
